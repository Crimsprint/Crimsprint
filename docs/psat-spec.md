# PSAT/NMSQT mode

Verified against College Board on November 22, 2026.

| Section | Modules | Questions per module | Minutes per module |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Reading and Writing | 2 | 27 (25 scored, 2 pretest) | 32 |
| Math | 2 | 22 (20 scored, 2 pretest) | 35 |

Both sections adapt their second module based on first-module performance. The timed sections total 134 minutes. The pretest questions are excluded from scoring. [PSAT/NMSQT structure](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/in-school-assessments/whats-on-the-test/psat-nmsqt/structure) · [Digital SAT Suite framework, table A29](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/assessment-framework-for-digital-sat-suite.pdf#page=185) · [Adaptive testing and scoring](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/scores/what-scores-mean/how-scores-calculated)

Reading and Writing and Math each range from 160 to 760. The total is their sum (320–1520). The Selection Index is (2 × Reading and Writing + Math) ÷ 10 and ranges from 48 to 228. [Fall 2026 PSAT/NMSQT Understanding Scores, p. 5](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/psat-nmsqt-understanding-scores.pdf#page=5)

SAT and PSAT/NMSQT share the four Reading and Writing and four Math content domains. Individual topics and domain weights can differ. For example, College Board lists circles and evaluating observational studies and experiments as SAT-only. Crimsprint uses the shared taxonomy and bank as requested, so PSAT practice can include SAT-only topics. [College Board content domains](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/higher-ed-professionals/sat-validity/content-domains) · [Digital SAT Suite framework, tables A30 and A32](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/assessment-framework-for-digital-sat-suite.pdf)

Crimsprint uses its existing estimated-score model with PSAT section limits and reports the Selection Index from the two estimated section scores. The difficulty mix is a tuning placeholder. The app labels the report “Estimated — not an official College Board score.”
