## Avatar and collaboration artwork

Create original avatar art for every catalog item, including collaborations. An item or coordinated set may not depict or closely imitate an existing character from a game, anime, movie, or comic. Check the combined silhouette, signature outfit, hair, accessories, and colors. Original designs can draw on broad genres and themes.
